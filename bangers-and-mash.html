Bangers and Mash: Traditional British Food

Discover bangers and mash, a classic British comfort meal made with sausages, mashed potatoes and commonly served with onion gravy.

Bangers and mash is a familiar British dish consisting of sausages served with mashed potatoes. It is commonly accompanied by onion gravy and may be served with peas or other vegetables.

The dish is associated with British home cooking, pubs and casual restaurant menus. Recipes and sausage varieties can vary considerably depending on the establishment.

What Is Bangers and Mash?

Bangers and mash combines cooked sausages with mashed potatoes. The simple combination makes it a popular example of British comfort food.

The term “bangers” is a traditional informal name for sausages. The dish can therefore appear on menus under different names while referring to the same basic combination.

Common Ingredients

A traditional-style serving may include:

  • British-style sausages.
  • Potatoes.
  • Butter or milk for mashed potatoes.
  • Onions.
  • Gravy or stock.
  • Peas or other vegetables.
  • Salt, pepper and other seasonings.

What Are Bangers?

“Bangers” is a traditional British term for sausages. British sausages come in many varieties and can differ in ingredients, seasoning and regional style.

The type of sausage used for bangers and mash therefore depends on the recipe or restaurant menu.

The Mashed Potatoes

Mashed potato provides the second main component of the dish. Potatoes are cooked and mashed, often with butter or milk, to create a soft and creamy side.

Some recipes use a simple mash, while others add seasoning or additional ingredients according to preference.

Onion Gravy

Onion gravy is a common accompaniment to bangers and mash. Sliced onions are cooked and combined with gravy or stock to create a savoury sauce.

The gravy is typically served over the sausages and mashed potatoes, adding flavour to the entire dish.

Vegetables Served with Bangers and Mash

Peas are a common side dish, although other vegetables may also be served. British pubs and restaurants can create their own combinations based on the menu.

The vegetables provide an additional element to the meal while keeping the main focus on sausages, mash and gravy.

Bangers and Mash on a British Menu

Bangers and mash is commonly found on pub and casual restaurant menus. It is often presented as a straightforward, hearty main course.

Why Is It Called Bangers and Mash?

The phrase combines the informal British term “bangers” for sausages with “mash”, referring to mashed potatoes.

The name is short, memorable and commonly associated with this particular combination of British foods.

Bangers and Mash in British Food Culture

Bangers and mash has become a familiar part of British comfort food culture. Its relatively simple ingredients make it suitable for home meals as well as pub and restaurant menus.

Modern versions can vary in sausage type, gravy, vegetables and presentation while retaining the basic identity of the dish.

Is Bangers and Mash Traditional British Food?

Yes. Bangers and mash is widely recognised as a traditional British comfort food and remains a familiar item on British pub and restaurant menus.

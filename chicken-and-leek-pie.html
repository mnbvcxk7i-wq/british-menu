Chicken and Leek Pie

A comforting British pie combining tender chicken, soft leeks and a rich savoury filling beneath a golden pastry crust.

What Is Chicken and Leek Pie?

Chicken and leek pie is a popular British savoury pie made with pieces of chicken and cooked leeks in a rich, creamy or savoury sauce. The filling is usually covered with pastry and baked until the crust becomes golden and crisp.

The mild flavour of leek works particularly well with chicken, creating a comforting combination that is suitable for family meals, lunches and traditional-style dinners.

Recipes can differ considerably. Some versions use a creamy sauce, while others use stock and herbs to create a lighter filling.

Traditional Chicken and Leek Pie Ingredients

The basic ingredients are straightforward and can be adapted depending on the recipe.

  • Chicken
  • Leeks
  • Pastry
  • Chicken stock
  • Cream or milk
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh or dried herbs

Some recipes also include mushrooms, carrots, peas, garlic or other vegetables to add extra flavour and texture.

The Chicken Filling

Chicken forms the main part of the filling. Cooked or gently simmered chicken is combined with the other ingredients before being placed into the pie.

The chicken is usually cut into bite-sized pieces so that each portion contains a good balance of meat and sauce.

The Role of Leeks

Leeks are an important ingredient because they provide a mild, slightly sweet onion-like flavour. When cooked slowly, leeks become soft and tender and blend naturally into the creamy filling.

Their delicate flavour makes them a suitable partner for chicken without overpowering the main ingredient.

The Pie Sauce

The filling is often held together by a thick sauce. A traditional style can be made using butter, flour and stock, with milk or cream added to create a richer texture.

Herbs and black pepper can also be used to add flavour. The sauce should be thick enough to sit inside the pastry without becoming overly liquid.

The Pastry Crust

Pastry gives the pie its characteristic golden exterior and adds texture to the soft filling. Puff pastry is often used for a light and flaky crust, while shortcrust pastry can create a firmer and more substantial finish.

Before baking, the pastry may be brushed with beaten egg to help produce a golden surface.

How Chicken and Leek Pie Is Served

Chicken and leek pie is commonly served warm as a main meal. Traditional accompaniments can include mashed potatoes, peas, green vegetables or other seasonal vegetables.

The creamy filling and crisp pastry make the dish suitable for colder days when hearty comfort food is especially popular.

Chicken and Leek Pie in British Food Culture

Chicken pies have long been a familiar part of British cooking, while leek is a well-known ingredient in British and Welsh culinary traditions. Combining the two creates a simple and adaptable savoury pie.

Today, chicken and leek pie can be found in home kitchens, bakeries, pubs, restaurants and supermarkets across the UK. Individual pies and larger family-sized versions are both common.

Popular Variations

Modern recipes can change the filling according to taste and available ingredients. Common additions include:

  • Mushrooms
  • Peas
  • Carrots
  • Garlic
  • Thyme
  • Parsley
  • Cheese

Some cooks also prepare the dish as individual pies, while others make one large pie designed to serve several people.

Chicken and Leek Pie vs Other British Pies

Chicken and leek pie has a lighter flavour profile than many beef pies because chicken and leeks are paired with a creamy or mild savoury sauce.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is chicken and leek pie made of?

It is generally made with chicken, leeks, pastry and a savoury sauce made from ingredients such as stock, butter, flour, milk or cream.

What pastry is best for chicken and leek pie?

Puff pastry and shortcrust pastry are both suitable. Puff pastry creates a lighter flaky crust, while shortcrust pastry provides a firmer texture.

Can vegetables be added to chicken and leek pie?

Yes. Mushrooms, peas, carrots and other vegetables can be added depending on the recipe.

Is chicken and leek pie served hot?

It is commonly served warm as a main meal, often with potatoes, peas or other vegetables.

Can chicken and leek pie be made as individual pies?

Yes. The filling can be divided into smaller portions and baked as individual pies instead of making one large pie.

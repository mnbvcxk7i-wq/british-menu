Bread and Butter Pudding

Bread and butter pudding is a classic British dessert made with buttered bread, dried fruit and a rich custard mixture. Discover its ingredients, preparation method and traditional serving ideas.

What Is Bread and Butter Pudding?

Bread and butter pudding is a traditional British dessert that turns simple slices of bread into a warm, comforting baked pudding. The bread is usually buttered and layered with dried fruit before being covered with a sweet custard mixture.

During baking, the bread absorbs the custard and develops a soft, rich centre with a lightly golden top. It is a well-known example of British home cooking that makes good use of everyday ingredients.

Traditional Ingredients

The basic recipe uses simple ingredients that are commonly found in a traditional British kitchen.

  • Slices of bread
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Raisins or other dried fruit
  • Nutmeg or cinnamon, if desired
  • Vanilla, depending on the recipe

Why Bread Is Used

Bread is the foundation of the pudding and absorbs the sweet custard during baking. Slightly stale bread can work particularly well because it can absorb liquid without falling apart too quickly.

White bread is common in traditional versions, although other breads can be used to create different textures and flavours.

The Custard Mixture

The custard is generally made by combining milk, eggs and sugar. Vanilla or spices can be added for additional flavour.

The mixture should be poured evenly over the bread so that the slices have enough time to absorb the liquid before baking. This creates the soft texture that distinguishes the pudding from ordinary toast or baked bread.

How to Make Bread and Butter Pudding

Begin by buttering slices of bread and arranging them in a baking dish. Scatter dried fruit between the layers so that it is distributed throughout the pudding.

Prepare the custard by combining the milk, eggs and sugar. Pour it over the bread and allow it to soak for a short time.

Sprinkle the top with a little sugar or spice if desired, then bake until the custard has set and the top has become golden.

How the Texture Develops

The finished pudding has a combination of textures. The centre is soft and custard-like because the bread absorbs the milk and egg mixture, while the upper surface becomes lightly crisp and golden.

The balance between the soft interior and browned top is an important part of the appeal of this traditional dessert.

Popular Flavour Additions

Raisins and sultanas are common additions, while nutmeg and cinnamon can provide gentle warmth. Some modern variations use citrus zest, chocolate, dried cranberries or other ingredients.

These additions can change the character of the pudding, but the classic version remains relatively simple and focuses on bread, butter, custard and dried fruit.

How to Serve Bread and Butter Pudding

Bread and butter pudding is often served warm. Custard is a traditional accompaniment and provides additional creaminess.

Cream or ice cream can also be served alongside the pudding. Because the dessert is already rich, a simple accompaniment often works well.

A Traditional British Comfort Dessert

Bread and butter pudding reflects an important tradition in British home cooking: making a satisfying dessert from inexpensive everyday ingredients.

Similar bread-based puddings have appeared in British cooking for generations, and recipes have changed over time according to the ingredients available in different households.

Tips for Making a Good Pudding

  • Use bread that is slightly firm rather than extremely fresh.
  • Spread the butter evenly over the bread.
  • Distribute dried fruit between the layers.
  • Pour the custard evenly over the entire dish.
  • Allow the bread time to absorb some of the custard.
  • Bake until the centre is set and the top is golden.
  • Serve warm with custard or another preferred accompaniment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is bread and butter pudding made from?

It is commonly made from buttered bread, milk, eggs, sugar and dried fruit, with spices or vanilla often added for flavour.

Is bread and butter pudding British?

Yes. Bread and butter pudding is a well-known traditional British dessert associated with home cooking.

What type of bread is best?

Traditional versions often use white bread, and slightly stale bread can absorb the custard particularly well.

What is usually served with bread and butter pudding?

Warm custard is a classic accompaniment, while cream or ice cream can also be served with the pudding.

Can other dried fruits be used?

Yes. Raisins and sultanas are traditional choices, but other dried fruits can be used for variations.

Privacy Policy

This page explains how British Menu approaches website information, technical data, cookies, external links and privacy-related matters.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to British Menu. We respect the privacy of visitors and aim to keep this website simple, useful and transparent. This privacy policy explains the general information practices that may apply when you visit and use this website.

British Menu is an informational website focused on British food, traditional dishes, menus, meal ideas, desserts, breakfast, lunch, dinner and related food topics.

Information We May Receive

British Menu does not currently require visitors to create an account or submit personal information simply to browse the website.

If you voluntarily contact us, information included in your message may be received so that we can understand and respond to your enquiry.

Technical Information

Like many websites, the services used to host and deliver a website may process basic technical information required to provide pages to visitors.

Depending on the hosting and technical services used, this can include information such as browser type, device information, approximate technical details, requested pages and other information associated with delivering web content.

This information is not intentionally used by British Menu to identify individual visitors for advertising or profiling.

How Information Is Used

Information that is voluntarily provided to British Menu may be used to:

  • Respond to questions or messages.
  • Review website suggestions and corrections.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems.
  • Improve the usefulness and quality of website content.
  • Maintain the security and normal operation of the website.

Cookies

British Menu is designed to be a lightweight informational website and does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or user profiling at present.

If analytics, advertising, embedded services or other third-party features are added in the future and those services use cookies or similar technologies, this privacy policy may be updated to explain the relevant practices.

Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party technical services to provide hosting, security, website delivery or other necessary functionality.

Third-party services operate under their own policies and terms. Where appropriate, visitors should review the privacy information provided by those services.

External Links

British Menu may contain links to external websites, restaurants, food resources or other online services.

Once you leave British Menu and visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms may apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites that are outside our control.

Children's Privacy

British Menu is a general informational website about food and menus. We do not intentionally request personal information from visitors simply for browsing the website.

Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information when contacting any website.

Data Security

We aim to keep the website and its supporting systems reasonably secure. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when website features, technical services or information practices change.

Any significant changes will be reflected on this page by updating the policy and its revision date.

Contact British Menu

If you have a question about this privacy policy or about information practices on the website, you can visit our Contact page.

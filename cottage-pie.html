Cottage Pie: Traditional British Dish

Discover Cottage Pie, a classic British comfort food made with seasoned minced beef, vegetables, gravy and a mashed potato topping.

Cottage Pie is a traditional British dish made with minced beef, vegetables and a savoury gravy filling covered with mashed potato. The dish is baked until the potato topping is lightly browned.

Cottage Pie is closely related to Shepherd's Pie. The main traditional difference is the meat used: Cottage Pie is associated with beef, while Shepherd's Pie is traditionally made with lamb or mutton.

What Is Cottage Pie?

Cottage Pie is a baked savoury dish consisting of a minced beef filling covered with mashed potatoes. The filling commonly includes onions, carrots, peas and gravy or stock.

It is often served as a hearty main meal and is associated with traditional British home cooking and comfort food.

Traditional Cottage Pie Ingredients

Recipes vary between households and restaurants, but common ingredients include:

  • Minced beef.
  • Potatoes.
  • Onions.
  • Carrots.
  • Peas.
  • Beef stock or gravy.
  • Herbs and seasonings.
  • Butter or milk for mashed potatoes.

Why Is It Called Cottage Pie?

The name Cottage Pie is traditionally associated with a simple pie-style dish made from readily available ingredients, particularly beef and potatoes.

The dish became associated with economical home cooking, making use of ingredients that were practical for households.

Cottage Pie vs Shepherd's Pie

Cottage Pie and Shepherd's Pie have a very similar structure: both usually contain a savoury minced-meat filling covered with mashed potato.

The Mashed Potato Topping

Mashed potato forms the top layer of Cottage Pie. The potatoes are normally cooked and mashed before being spread over the savoury beef filling.

When baked, the surface can become lightly browned and develop a pleasant texture. Some recipes create ridges in the potato topping before baking.

Vegetables in Cottage Pie

Onions, carrots and peas are commonly used in Cottage Pie. They are cooked together with the minced beef and gravy to create the filling.

Modern recipes may include additional vegetables according to personal preference or the style of the restaurant.

How Cottage Pie Is Served

Cottage Pie is generally served hot as a main course. It can be prepared in one large baking dish or divided into individual portions.

Additional vegetables, greens or other side dishes can be served with it depending on the menu.

Cottage Pie on a British Menu

Cottage Pie can be found on menus featuring traditional British food, particularly in pubs and restaurants serving hearty comfort dishes.

Restaurant versions may vary in seasoning, vegetables, portion size and presentation, while retaining the familiar combination of minced beef and mashed potato.

Is Cottage Pie a Traditional British Dish?

Yes. Cottage Pie is widely recognised as a traditional British dish and is associated with home cooking, comfort food and traditional British menus.

Cottage Pie is traditionally covered with mashed potato before it is baked.

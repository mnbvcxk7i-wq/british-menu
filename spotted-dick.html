Spotted Dick: Traditional British Pudding

Learn about Spotted Dick, a traditional British steamed pudding made with dried fruit and commonly served with custard.

Spotted Dick is a traditional British steamed pudding made with flour, dried fruit and a rich pudding mixture. It is commonly served warm with custard.

The pudding is particularly recognisable for the dried fruit distributed throughout the steamed mixture, creating the characteristic "spotted" appearance.

What Is Spotted Dick?

Spotted Dick is a classic British steamed pudding traditionally containing dried currants or other dried fruit.

Unlike baked sponge desserts, the pudding is traditionally prepared using steaming, which gives it a soft and dense texture.

Common Spotted Dick Ingredients

Traditional-style recipes commonly include:

  • Plain flour.
  • Dried currants or other dried fruit.
  • Suet or a suitable alternative.
  • Sugar.
  • Milk or another liquid ingredient.
  • Baking ingredients.
  • Optional lemon zest or other flavouring.

The Dried Fruit

Dried fruit is one of the defining ingredients in Spotted Dick. Currants are traditionally associated with the pudding and are distributed throughout the mixture.

The fruit provides sweetness and creates the small darker spots that are associated with the pudding's name.

What Does "Spotted" Mean?

The word "spotted" refers to the dried fruit visible throughout the pudding. The fruit creates small dark spots in the pale steamed mixture.

How Spotted Dick Is Cooked

Spotted Dick is traditionally steamed rather than baked. Steaming allows the pudding mixture to cook gradually while retaining moisture.

The exact preparation method and cooking time can vary between recipes.

How Spotted Dick Is Served

Spotted Dick is usually served warm. Custard is the most familiar accompaniment, although cream or other sauces may also be served.

Component Common Choice

Spotted Dick and Custard

Warm custard is a classic accompaniment to Spotted Dick. Its smooth texture complements the dense steamed pudding.

The combination of warm pudding and custard is representative of traditional British pudding service.

Spotted Dick in British Food Culture

Spotted Dick is associated with traditional British cooking and is one of the country's well-known steamed puddings.

It can be found in traditional food discussions and, depending on the establishment, on British pub or restaurant dessert menus.

Spotted Dick vs Sticky Toffee Pudding

Both are warm British desserts, but they differ in their main ingredients and preparation methods.

Feature Spotted Dick Sticky Toffee Pudding

Sauce Often served with custard Toffee sauce

Variations of Spotted Dick

Modern recipes may make adjustments to the traditional ingredients while keeping the basic idea of a steamed fruit pudding.

  • Different dried fruits.
  • Alternative fats instead of traditional suet.
  • Lemon or orange zest.
  • Different custard recipes.
  • Individual steamed portions.

Is Spotted Dick a Traditional British Dessert?

Yes. Spotted Dick is widely recognised as a traditional British steamed pudding and is part of the country's long-established pudding tradition.

Ploughman's Pie

Ploughman's pie brings together familiar British flavours inspired by the classic Ploughman's lunch, combining savoury pastry with ingredients such as cheese, onion and chutney.

What Is Ploughman's Pie?

Ploughman's pie is a savoury pie inspired by the ingredients and flavours associated with a traditional Ploughman's lunch. The combination commonly centres around mature cheese, onions and chutney, with pastry providing a crisp and filling outer layer.

It is a useful example of how familiar British ingredients can be combined into a warm baked dish. Recipes can vary considerably, with some versions using additional vegetables or different varieties of cheese.

Ingredients Commonly Used

There is no single fixed recipe for Ploughman's pie, but several ingredients work particularly well with its traditional flavour profile.

  • Shortcrust or other savoury pastry
  • Mature Cheddar or another strong British cheese
  • Onions
  • Pickle or chutney
  • Potatoes or other vegetables, depending on the recipe
  • Black pepper and simple seasoning
  • Egg or milk for glazing the pastry, if desired

The Flavours of a Ploughman's Lunch

The idea behind this pie comes from the familiar combination found in a Ploughman's lunch. Cheese provides richness, onions add a savoury bite and pickle or chutney contributes sweetness and acidity.

Putting these flavours inside pastry creates a warmer and more substantial interpretation of the same general combination.

Choosing the Cheese

Mature Cheddar is a natural choice because its strong flavour can stand up to pastry, onions and pickle. A farmhouse-style Cheddar can provide an especially rich savoury character.

Cheese with a firm texture is also useful because it can retain some character during cooking instead of disappearing completely into the filling.

Pastry for Ploughman's Pie

Shortcrust pastry is a practical option for this type of savoury pie. It provides a crisp and buttery exterior while allowing the cheese and other filling ingredients to remain the main flavours.

The pastry should be rolled evenly and handled carefully so that it forms a secure covering. A small opening in the top can help steam escape while the pie cooks.

How to Prepare Ploughman's Pie

Prepare the pastry and filling separately before assembling the pie. Cheese, cooked onions and the chosen vegetables can be combined with a suitable amount of pickle or chutney.

Place the filling into a prepared pastry case or pie dish and cover it with pastry. Seal the edges, add a small steam opening and brush the surface with egg or milk if a golden finish is desired.

Bake until the pastry is crisp and golden and the filling is hot. Allow the pie to rest briefly before serving so the filling can settle.

Serving Suggestions

Ploughman's pie can be served warm with vegetables, peas or a simple salad. Extra chutney or pickle can also be served on the side for people who enjoy a stronger sweet and tangy flavour.

For a traditional British-style meal, it can be paired with simple seasonal vegetables and a light accompaniment rather than a heavily flavoured sauce.

Can You Make It Ahead?

A savoury pie can be convenient for preparing ahead of time. Preparing the filling earlier can make final assembly easier, while the pastry can be prepared separately and kept chilled until needed.

If preparing food ahead, allow cooked ingredients to cool appropriately before assembling the pie and follow safe food storage practices.

Ploughman's Pie and Other British Pies

British cuisine has a long tradition of savoury pies. Steak and kidney pie, steak and ale pie, chicken and leek pie and fish pie each use different fillings and cooking methods.

Ploughman's pie stands apart because its flavour combination is closely associated with the ingredients of the Ploughman's lunch, particularly cheese and pickle or chutney.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is usually in a Ploughman's pie?

Common ingredients include mature cheese, onions, pickle or chutney and savoury pastry. Other vegetables can be added depending on the recipe.

What cheese works best in Ploughman's pie?

Mature Cheddar is a popular option because it has a strong savoury flavour that works well with pastry, onions and chutney.

Can I use chutney in the pie?

Yes. Chutney or pickle can provide the sweet and tangy element associated with the flavours of a Ploughman's lunch.

What pastry is suitable for this pie?

Shortcrust pastry is a suitable choice for a savoury Ploughman's pie because it creates a crisp and sturdy crust.

What can I serve with Ploughman's pie?

It can be served with seasonal vegetables, peas, salad, chutney or pickle depending on the style of meal.

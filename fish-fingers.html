Fish Fingers

Fish fingers are a familiar British food made from portions of fish covered with a crisp coating. Learn about their ingredients, preparation, serving ideas and place in British food culture.

What Are Fish Fingers?

Fish fingers are rectangular portions of fish covered with a breadcrumb or batter-style coating. They are a popular convenience food in Britain and are commonly served as part of a quick family meal.

Their simple shape makes them easy to prepare and serve. They can be cooked in an oven, air fryer or other suitable cooking method according to the product or recipe being used.

Common Ingredients

Traditional-style fish fingers use a small number of basic ingredients. The exact ingredients vary depending on the recipe or product.

  • White fish such as cod or haddock
  • Breadcrumbs or another crisp coating
  • Flour
  • Egg
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional herbs and simple seasonings

What Fish Is Used?

White fish is commonly used because it has a mild flavour and a firm texture. Cod and haddock are familiar choices, although other suitable fish can also be used.

The fish should be prepared into portions that are thick enough to remain moist inside while the coating becomes crisp during cooking.

How Fish Fingers Are Coated

A simple homemade coating can be made by passing pieces of fish through flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs. The breadcrumbs create the characteristic crisp outer layer.

Seasonings can be added to the flour or breadcrumbs to provide additional flavour. The coating should cover the fish evenly so that it cooks consistently.

How to Cook Fish Fingers

Fish fingers can be cooked using several methods. Oven cooking is a common option because it provides an even heat while requiring little attention.

Air frying can also produce a crisp coating. When cooking packaged fish fingers, follow the cooking instructions provided on the packaging because cooking times and temperatures can differ.

How to Make Homemade Fish Fingers

To make homemade fish fingers, cut boneless white fish into similarly sized strips. Prepare separate dishes containing flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.

Coat each fish portion in flour, dip it into the egg and then cover it with breadcrumbs. Place the coated pieces on a suitable baking tray and cook until the coating is crisp and the fish is properly cooked through.

Always make sure fish is thoroughly cooked before serving.

Popular Ways to Serve Fish Fingers

Fish fingers can be served with chips, peas and tartare sauce for a simple British-style meal. They can also be placed inside a sandwich or served with salad for a lighter option.

Children and adults may enjoy different combinations, which is one reason fish fingers have remained a familiar part of everyday British meals.

Fish Fingers and British Food Culture

Fish fingers became a recognisable part of British convenience food during the twentieth century. Their convenient shape, relatively quick preparation and mild flavour helped make them familiar in British households.

They are also connected to Britain's wider tradition of eating fish, which includes dishes such as fish and chips and fish pie.

Fish Fingers vs Fish and Chips

Fish fingers and fish and chips both feature fish with a crisp outer coating, but they are prepared differently. Fish and chips traditionally uses a larger piece of fish with a batter coating, served alongside chips.

Fish fingers are smaller portions with a breadcrumb or similar coating and are often designed for quick preparation.

Tips for Better Fish Fingers

  • Cut homemade fish portions to a similar size.
  • Keep the coating evenly distributed.
  • Do not overcrowd the cooking tray.
  • Cook until the coating is crisp and the fish is fully cooked.
  • Serve soon after cooking for the best texture.
  • Use a suitable sauce such as tartare sauce for extra flavour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fish is commonly used in fish fingers?

Cod and haddock are common choices, although other suitable white fish can also be used.

Are fish fingers a British food?

Fish fingers are strongly associated with everyday British food culture and have been a familiar convenience food in the UK for many years.

What are fish fingers coated with?

They are commonly coated with breadcrumbs, although some versions use other crisp coatings.

What can I serve with fish fingers?

Chips, peas, salad, tartare sauce and bread are common accompaniments.

Can fish fingers be homemade?

Yes. Homemade fish fingers can be prepared by cutting white fish into strips and coating them with flour, egg and breadcrumbs before cooking.

Traditional British Food

Kedgeree

A distinctive British rice dish combining smoked fish, cooked rice, eggs and aromatic seasonings.

What Is Kedgeree?

Kedgeree is a traditional British dish made with cooked rice, smoked fish and eggs. It is usually seasoned with spices and herbs, creating a savoury dish with a distinctive combination of flavours.

Kedgeree became particularly associated with British cooking and was traditionally served as a breakfast or brunch dish. Today it is also enjoyed as a lunch or light main meal.

The dish is notable for combining ingredients and cooking influences that developed through the historical connections between Britain and the Indian subcontinent.

Traditional Kedgeree Ingredients

Kedgeree recipes vary, but several ingredients are commonly associated with the dish.

  • Cooked rice
  • Smoked fish
  • Hard-boiled eggs
  • Butter
  • Onion
  • Curry powder or spices
  • Parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Some recipes use cream or milk to create a richer texture, while others keep the rice relatively light and dry.

The Smoked Fish

Smoked fish is one of the defining ingredients of kedgeree. Smoked haddock is particularly associated with traditional British versions of the dish.

The fish is cooked separately or prepared before being flaked into pieces and mixed with the seasoned rice. Its smoky, savoury flavour provides a strong contrast to the mild rice and eggs.

The Role of Rice

Rice forms the main base of kedgeree. Cooked rice is combined with onions, seasonings and the flaked fish to create a filling dish.

The rice also helps distribute the flavours throughout the meal, allowing the smoked fish, spices and herbs to be enjoyed together.

Eggs in Kedgeree

Hard-boiled eggs are a familiar feature of traditional kedgeree. They are usually cut into pieces or arranged over the rice before serving.

The mild flavour and soft texture of the egg work well with the smoky fish and seasoned rice.

Spices and Seasonings

Kedgeree is commonly seasoned with curry powder or a combination of spices. This gives the dish a warm, aromatic character.

Onions, parsley, black pepper and butter can also be used to add additional flavour. The exact seasoning depends on the recipe.

Kedgeree and British Food History

Kedgeree has an interesting place in British food history because its development is connected with the colonial-era exchange of culinary traditions between Britain and the Indian subcontinent.

A related rice-and-lentil dish known as khichdi was adapted in different ways, and British versions developed into the kedgeree associated with smoked fish and eggs.

Over time, kedgeree became established as a recognisable dish in British cooking, particularly as a breakfast and brunch food.

How Kedgeree Is Served

Kedgeree is generally served warm. The cooked rice is mixed with the smoked fish and seasonings, while the eggs are incorporated into the dish or arranged on top.

Fresh parsley or other herbs can be added before serving. Some versions are served with lemon wedges or additional seasoning.

Kedgeree as a Breakfast Dish

Kedgeree has a long association with British breakfast tables. Its combination of rice, fish and eggs makes it more substantial than many conventional breakfast foods.

Although it is no longer an everyday breakfast for most people, kedgeree remains a familiar traditional British dish and is also suitable for brunch and lunch.

Popular Kedgeree Variations

Modern cooks can adapt kedgeree while keeping its basic character. Variations may include different types of smoked fish, additional vegetables or changes to the seasoning.

  • Smoked haddock kedgeree
  • Kedgeree with extra herbs
  • Creamy kedgeree
  • Kedgeree with peas
  • Modern vegetable variations

The choice of spices can also be changed to create a milder or more aromatic version.

Kedgeree vs Other British Breakfast Foods

Kedgeree is quite different from a Full English breakfast because it centres on rice, smoked fish and eggs rather than sausages, bacon, eggs, beans and toast.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is kedgeree made of?

Kedgeree is commonly made with cooked rice, smoked fish, hard-boiled eggs, onions, butter and aromatic seasonings.

What fish is traditionally used in kedgeree?

Smoked haddock is particularly associated with traditional British kedgeree.

Is kedgeree a breakfast dish?

Yes. Kedgeree became particularly associated with British breakfast and brunch, although it can also be served as a lunch or light main meal.

Does kedgeree contain curry?

Many recipes use curry powder or similar spices, giving the dish its characteristic aromatic flavour.

Are eggs used in kedgeree?

Yes. Hard-boiled eggs are a traditional and recognisable part of many kedgeree recipes.

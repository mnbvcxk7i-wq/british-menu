Our Purpose

A Guide to British Food and Menus

British Menu is an informational website focused on traditional British food, classic dishes, meal ideas and different types of menus associated with British cuisine.

British food has developed over many generations and includes a wide variety of breakfasts, lunches, dinners, pub meals, pies, roasts, seafood dishes, puddings and desserts.

Our website brings information about these foods together in an easy-to-read format so visitors can explore different parts of British cuisine.

  • British lunch and dinner foods
  • Classic British pub food

  • British menu ideas

Information Approach

Clear and Useful Food Information

We aim to present British food information in a clear and accessible way. Food traditions can vary between regions, families and individual restaurants, so a dish may have different ingredients or preparation methods.

Where appropriate, our content distinguishes traditional descriptions from modern variations so readers can better understand the foods they are exploring.

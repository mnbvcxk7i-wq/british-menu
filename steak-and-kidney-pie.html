Steak and Kidney Pie: Traditional British Pie

Learn about steak and kidney pie, a classic British savoury pie made with meat, rich gravy and pastry.

Steak and kidney pie is a well-known British savoury pie traditionally made with pieces of beef, kidney and a rich savoury gravy enclosed in pastry.

It has a long association with British home cooking, pubs and traditional British menus. It can be served as a substantial main meal with potatoes, vegetables or other traditional accompaniments.

What Is Steak and Kidney Pie?

Steak and kidney pie combines pieces of beef and kidney with onions, gravy and seasoning. The filling is enclosed in pastry and baked until the pastry is cooked and golden.

Some versions use pastry mainly as a lid over a dish of filling, while other versions are completely enclosed in pastry.

Common Ingredients

Traditional versions can vary, but the main ingredients often include:

  • Beef steak.
  • Kidney.
  • Onions.
  • Beef stock or gravy.
  • Wheat flour or another thickening ingredient.
  • Pastry.
  • Seasonings and herbs.

The Steak Filling

Beef provides the main meaty component of the pie. It is generally cooked together with the gravy so that the filling becomes rich and tender.

The exact cut and preparation of beef can differ between recipes, restaurants and regional versions.

Kidney in the Pie

Kidney is the ingredient that gives this pie its traditional name and distinctive character. Beef kidney is commonly associated with classic versions.

The amount of kidney can vary depending on the recipe and personal preference.

Rich British Gravy

The meat and kidney are traditionally combined with a savoury gravy. The gravy helps keep the filling moist and brings the ingredients together.

Onions, stock and seasoning are commonly used to build the flavour of the filling.

Pastry and Pie Crust

Pastry is an important part of steak and kidney pie. The crust provides a crisp outer layer while the filling remains warm and savoury underneath.

Different recipes may use shortcrust or puff pastry depending on the desired texture and style.

Steak and Kidney Pie in British Pubs

Steak and kidney pie has a strong connection with British pub food. Traditional pubs may serve savoury pies alongside chips, peas, vegetables and gravy.

Pie dishes are particularly associated with hearty British meals and casual dining.

Steak and Kidney Pie as a British Dinner

The pie can be served as a main meal at lunch or dinner. A typical plate may include the pie together with potatoes, vegetables and gravy.

The combination of pastry, savoury filling and traditional side dishes makes it suitable for a classic British menu.

Is Steak and Kidney Pie Traditional British Food?

Steak and kidney pie is widely recognised as a traditional British savoury dish. It has been associated with British home cooking, pub menus and classic pie dishes for generations.

Modern recipes may change the pastry, seasoning or proportions, but the combination of steak, kidney, gravy and pastry remains central to the traditional style.

British Food Culture

Savoury pies have an important place in British food culture. They are found in home cooking, pubs, cafés and traditional restaurant menus.

Steak and kidney pie is particularly associated with the traditional British pub meal, where it can be served with chips, peas and gravy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is steak and kidney pie made of?

It is traditionally made with beef steak, kidney, gravy, onions, seasoning and pastry.

What type of kidney is used in steak and kidney pie?

Traditional versions are commonly associated with beef kidney, although recipes can vary.

What pastry is used for steak and kidney pie?

Recipes may use shortcrust or puff pastry. The choice depends on the recipe and desired texture.

What is served with steak and kidney pie?

Common accompaniments include chips, mashed potatoes, peas, vegetables and gravy.

Is steak and kidney pie a British dish?

Yes. It is widely associated with traditional British cuisine and classic pub and home cooking.
